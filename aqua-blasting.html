Aqua Blasting

Also known as vapour blasting. Fine media carried in water cleans metal gently, leaving a smooth satin, factory-fresh finish on aluminium and delicate parts.

How it works

Water cushions the media as it hits the surface, so grime, oxidation and old finishes come off without pitting or roughing up the metal the way dry blasting can.

The result is a consistent, satin, as-cast look. It's the go-to for engine cases and aluminium, and it also works as a gentle prep before coating.

Good for

  • Engine cases and clutch covers
  • Carburettors and cylinder heads
  • Fork lowers and aluminium castings
  • Delicate or thin parts
  • Aqua blast only, or aqua blast & coat
