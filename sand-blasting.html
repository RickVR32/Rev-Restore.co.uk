Sand Blasting

Rust, scale, old paint and tired powder coat stripped back to clean bare metal, ready for a new finish or left raw.

How it works

Abrasive media is fired at the surface under pressure, cutting through rust and coatings without the hours of sanding and chemical stripping.

It leaves a clean, slightly keyed surface, which is exactly what powder coat needs to grip. Most jobs are blast and coat, but blast-only is available too.

Good for

  • Rust and scale removal
  • Stripping old paint and powder coat
  • Steel wheels and hubs
  • Brackets, mounts and suspension arms
  • Rusty fasteners and small chassis parts
  • Prep before powder coating
