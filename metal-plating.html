Metal Plating

Gold, chrome, zinc and nickel finishes — plating that protects the part as well as finishing it, on components that take a genuine hammering out on the road.

SERVICE HERO IMAGE
search: "chrome plated motorcycle parts"

What's included

Each finish suits different needs: chrome for classic durability and shine, zinc for corrosion protection on fasteners and brackets, nickel as an underlayer or standalone satin finish, and gold for a distinctive, harder-to-find option.

Bring a specific finish in mind or talk through what suits the part — some components (fasteners especially) are better suited to zinc than chrome for long-term durability.
