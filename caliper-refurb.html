Caliper Refurbishment & Painting

Brake calipers stripped, rebuilt and finished properly — not just sprayed over old seals and corrosion.

What's included

Calipers are fully stripped, checked for wear and corrosion, rebuilt with new seals where needed, and finished in your choice of colour. This isn't a cosmetic-only service — function gets checked before anything goes back on the bike.

A common add-on alongside a full restoration or a suspension rebuild, since calipers are already off the bike at that point.
