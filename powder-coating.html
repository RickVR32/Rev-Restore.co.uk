Powder Coating

A tough, precise finish for frames, swingarms and small components — stripped, prepped and coated properly, not just sprayed over the top of the last owner's mistakes.

What's included

Every part is fully stripped back before it goes anywhere near the booth — old coating, rust and contamination removed so the new finish actually bonds, rather than sitting on top of the problem. Once prepped, parts are coated and cured to a durable, chip-resistant finish available in gloss, satin or textured options.

Suitable for frames, swingarms, yokes, wheels hubs, brackets and most small components. Bring a colour reference or leave it open and we'll talk through what suits the bike.
