Vapour Blasting

The raw, satin-aluminium look for engine cases, fork lowers and casings — stripped back to bare metal without the aggressive texture of dry blasting.

SERVICE HERO IMAGE
search: "vapour blasting engine case"

What's included

Vapour blasting uses a wet media process that strips grime, old finish and light corrosion without etching or roughing up the base metal the way dry blasting can. What's left is a consistent, satin, as-cast look.

This is the go-to for engine cases, clutch covers, fork lowers and any aluminium part where you want it looking clean and factory-fresh rather than coloured or plated.
