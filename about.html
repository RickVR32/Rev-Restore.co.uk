The Workshop

One person. One standard.

Rev-Restore is a one-person workshop — every job that comes through the door gets worked on personally, start to finish.

Why work with a one-person workshop

There's no handoff between departments here — the person who quotes the job is the person who strips it, finishes it, and hands it back. That means fewer miscommunications, and a level of care that's harder to keep consistent once a job passes through several sets of hands.

It also means capacity is limited by design. Jobs are taken on at a pace that keeps quality consistent, rather than stacking up a queue that compromises the finish on any single piece.

Every part is properly stripped and prepped before anything else happens to it — that's the difference between a finish that lasts and one that's just papering over the problem.
