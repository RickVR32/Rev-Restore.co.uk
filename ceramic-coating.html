Ceramic / Heat Coating

Heat-resistant ceramic coating for exhausts and headers — protects against discolouration and corrosion while keeping the finish looking right long after a chrome or paint job would have gone.

What's included

Ceramic coating is baked on and built specifically to handle sustained high heat without the blueing, staining or flaking that chrome and standard paint suffer from on headers and exhaust systems.

Available in a range of finishes from matte black through to polished tones — suited to both daily riders and show-quality builds.
