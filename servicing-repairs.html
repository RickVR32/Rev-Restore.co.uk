Motorbike Servicing & Repairs

Routine servicing and mechanical repair work, carried out in the same workshop as the finishing side of things — so a bike coming in for a service can flag up anything worth addressing at the same time.

What's included

Standard servicing covers the usual — oil and filter changes, chain and sprocket checks, brake inspection, fluid levels and general condition checks — plus mechanical repair work as needed.

If something turns up during a service that's worth a proper fix, you'll get a call before any extra work goes ahead — no surprise costs on the invoice.
