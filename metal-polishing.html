Metal Polishing

A true mirror finish on aluminium and steel components — worked back from raw, oxidised or corroded stock rather than just buffed over the surface.

What's included

Parts are worked through a full sequence of abrasive stages before final buffing, so pitting and oxidation are actually removed rather than masked. The result holds its shine rather than dulling back within weeks.

Suitable for yokes, engine covers, wheel rims, foot pegs, levers and most aluminium or steel components not going for a powder-coated finish.
